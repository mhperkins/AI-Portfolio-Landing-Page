Text source for the three priority application resumes. Edit here, then run
`python build_priority_resumes.py`. Each `# RESUME:` block builds
`Maxwell Perkins Resume - <name>.docx` in the academic CV's format. Everything from `# NOTES` on is
ignored by the build.

Line types: `Contact:` and `Links:` make the header; `## HEADING`; `**Title** | meta | meta` is an
entry; `*text*` is an italic note; `- text` is a bullet (`- **Lead:** text` bolds the lead);
`**Label:** text` is a plain labeled line.

## Why each resume is shaped this way

**Shared calls.** Header reads "Madison, WI" (Max can interview in person on a week's notice and start
Oct 19). Community Manager is the real title. Handshake AI and the PMP are left off. Sprout bullets
follow each posting's own priority order, not what sounds biggest. The numbers match the letters:
around 3,000 donors, over $1.5 million raised, one showcase a month to 10 to 15 events, over 100 new
people a month. Teaching drops from the top section to one line, since none of the three needs it.

**Savanna Institute (manager).** Experience first, in the order of Savanna's percentages: advancement
operations (donor records, communications), then grants, then project and vendor coordination, then
community growth. Founder entry at full weight (two bullets) to carry the three-years requirement.
Applied Systems gets all four tools, with the one AI line, because Savanna asks for automation, email
marketing, a CMS, and design. "Over $1.5 million raised" sits in the Sprout note so it reads as the
organization's total. Tools add Campaign Monitor and website design and maintenance (Savanna prefers
email marketing and CMS experience). The Interests line answers Savanna's preferred "interest in
agriculture, conservation, climate, or environmental nonprofits". Portfolio link on.

**River Alliance and MEA (assistant).** Lowest altitude of the three: "enter", "maintain", "support",
"coordinate". Bullets follow the posting: donor data, donor communications, events with partners,
admin support, network, grants. The founder entry moves to the bottom at one line (the arts-education
track's flight-risk rule for hourly seats). Applied Systems is one line with the AI mention, next to
the letter's water paragraph. Interests carries the required commitment to water conservation.
Availability states the Oct 19 in-person start. Portfolio link off, matching the letter (open call).

**WFAA (coordinator).** Education first, with UW-Madison on top of NYU on purpose (breaks reverse
chronology for an alumni audience), and the rugby title and Union shows under it. Sprout bullets follow
WFAA's four areas in order: program operations (onboarding), events and constituent experience,
constituent relations and records, project coordination and communications. No AI wording, matching
Max's own edit of the WFAA letter, which took the AI line out. Three tools, not the Grant Tool, since
WFAA's work is programs and events. Portfolio link on, labeled "Portfolio".
