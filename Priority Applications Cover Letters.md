## The template

Five paragraphs. Bracketed slots change per job; the rest holds.

1. **[HOOK]** Why this mission, through a true personal tie.
2. **[BREADTH]** Music Major Records, then the range of Sprout Society work. Volume per job.
3. **CORE PROOF** (shared) The CRM, the donor base, the communications, and the one AI line.
4. **[FIT]** The job's own asks, in its own order. For Savanna, the remaining required qualifications.
5. **CLOSE** Support their team; logistics the posting asks about.
