Parallel track to `career-search-master.md`. That doc covers AI/dev roles. This one covers
operations, events, administration, and assistant roles in creative and nonprofit organizations.

---

## Hard Rules For This Track

- **AI/vibe-coding disclosure is case by case, not blanket-banned (updated Sept 4, 2026).**
  Default is still to omit it for traditional arts/nonprofit ops roles: the art world is often
  hostile to it and it can read as a flight risk. Translate to "I built systems that made us
  faster and more accurate." But when the org or role is itself tech- or AI-adjacent (digital
  fluency listed as a plus, a media/tech-forward organization, an academic
  technology-and-AI post), surface the portfolio and lead with the AI-assisted tool building as a
  differentiator instead of hiding it. Decide per posting, not by default.
- **No em dashes.**
- **Concise and narrative driven.** Not the resume in prose. Lead with what you want to do, use
  past experience only as the evidence that you can do it.
- **Do not claim years you do not have.** Sprout Society is one year. Win on scope and ownership,
  not tenure.
- **Software Developer Resume.pdf will undercut every letter on this track.** A parallel
  operations resume is required before sending.

## The Story

**One-liner:** I'm a composer who runs the operations of a creative space, so the artists and
organizers around me can do the part only they can do.

**The arc, three beats:**

1. **Fifteen years inside the work.** Master's in composition, pianist and drummer, performing
   artist, producer, teacher. You understand creative people because you are one.
2. **Then you learned the business side by building one.** Music Major Records, 2023 to 2025.
   You ran it, scoped it honestly, and set it down to go get real operating experience. Frame as
   judgment, never as failure.
3. **Now you run a creative organization.** Sprout Society, team of three, one year, every hat.
   Community management, events, space, grants, donor communication, the founder's calendar and
   inbox. You found the revenue problem before anyone asked and fixed it.

**The differentiator:** most applicants for these roles are organized but not creative, or
creative but not organized. You ran the numbers *and* host the showcase.

## Cover Letter Formula For This Track

1. **What you want**, stated plainly, in their words.
2. **The scope paragraph.** Sprout Society, ordered to match their priority list.
3. **The proactivity story.** The revenue model. This is the strongest paragraph you have.
4. **Why you specifically.** Artist plus entrepreneur plus teacher.
5. **Logistics and close.** One or two lines.

Target 280-320 words.
