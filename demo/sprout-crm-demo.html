How Claude helps with outreach

Claude works from an outreach protocol written in plain language, so changing how Sprout reaches out means editing a document, not code. Connected to the CRM through a custom MCP server, it:

  1. Finds people and groups worth knowing: new followers, local collectives, past guests.
  2. Checks the CRM so nobody is added twice, then researches fit and sorts each into Tier A, B, or C.
  3. Drafts a first message in Sprout's voice and files the brief here to edit, rewrite, or skip. Nothing sends on its own.
