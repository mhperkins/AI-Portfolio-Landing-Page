## RESEARCH

**Research focus:** AI's most established application in music is generative synthesis, composition and audio models trained to produce musical output. My research asks where else AI has utility in music: evaluation, analysis, and pedagogy, domains where the field has little established methodology. The Composer Compass is the first sustained project built to test that question.

### The Composer Compass (2026-present)
A structured protocol for training and evaluating AI systems on compositional work, paired with the composition tool built to run it.

- Designed a multi-phase agent training protocol: an eight-section intake form for each piece, a system-prompt-driven analysis agent connected via the Model Context Protocol, and a variable-ceiling evaluation rubric scoring compositional output across six dimensions plus three modifiers.
- Indexed a personal music theory library into a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline (ChromaDB, sentence-transformer embeddings), so the evaluation agent grounds its analysis in reference material rather than an unexamined model prior.
- Ran a structured experiment protocol scoring compositional output from multiple model configurations against the rubric, producing a comparative record of where different AI systems succeed and fail at music composition.
- **Relevance:** this defines what "good" AI-assisted composition means in measurable terms and builds the infrastructure to test that definition at scale, evaluation and systems-design work, not just generative tool use. That is the same work a music technology and AI curriculum needs to teach.
