## CREATIVE WORK & RESEARCH: THE COMPOSER HUB

Current AI music tools are built to generate music, offering nothing to a musician who already knows how to write and make it. The Composer Hub is built to prove AI can assist trained musicians in their creative process, career, and workflow, without diluting human creation.

### The Composer Compass (2026-present)
*An AI composer assistant that does not compose music; it learns your compositional process and augments your creative capacity through a structured protocol in the background and a simple interface in the foreground.*

- Learns a composer's process from a structured intake form filled out per piece, then evaluates new work against it: trained via an AI analysis agent and a rubric scoring six compositional dimensions plus three modifiers.
- Grounds every evaluation in the composer's own reference material, not generic AI assumptions, via a searchable personal music theory library (ChromaDB).
- Allows the composer to visualize their creative workflow in a Next.js interface built on the Anthropic API: planning, sketching, analyzing, draft management, and engraving.
- Tested the trained agent across several AI models to compare the utility of each response using a multi-dimension, weighted rubric.

### Composer CRM (2026-present)
A tool to organize and manage the career side of being a composer.

- Tracks performers, prospects, ensembles, venues, and concerts (budgets, communications, media).
- A newsletter builder tool.
- An AI outreach feature to research and compile new prospects.

### Virtual Agency (2026-present, in training)
*A virtual label/publisher: customizable AI employees that fill the roles a record label or publisher normally provides, still in training and under close human oversight.*

- Each agent is a system prompt (job description) plus a set of tools/skills.
- Can run on a schedule or be triggered manually.
- Marketing, Web Design, and Social Media virtual employees have produced a concert landing page and digital program notes, an outreach campaign, and a content calendar.
