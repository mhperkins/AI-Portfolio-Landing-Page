## Hard Rules For This Track

- **Lead with The Composer Compass, not Sprout Society.** The creative/research work is the focal
  point. Sprout Society and Music Major Records are professional experience, listed but secondary.
- **Lead with confidence, not apology, on the non-traditional path.** Music technology and AI is
  new enough that it has no established academic pipeline yet, which means practical, hands-on
  systems-building experience is a legitimate primary qualification, not a workaround for missing
  a doctorate. State the M.M. and the lack of university classroom teaching as facts, once, without
  hedging language ("I want to state plainly," "I take this gap seriously"). Never imply a research
  record or teaching history that does not exist, but never frame the real record as a deficiency
  either.
- **The portfolio comes out for this track.** Unlike the ops track's default, an academic
  technology-and-AI post is exactly the audience the portfolio was built for. Link it in the cover
  letter.
- **No em dashes.**

## The Story

**One-liner:** A composer who builds the actual AI systems used to make and evaluate music, not
someone writing about AI in music from outside it.

**The angle:** RISE-AI is an interdisciplinary AI hiring initiative. The typical competing
candidate has a DMA or PhD and a conventional publication/performance record. The honest
differentiator is not competing on that axis, it is The Composer Compass: a working, independently
built AI training-and-evaluation protocol for composition, which is functionally a research
project even though it was not produced through an academic pipeline.

**What to not do:** do not pad the CV to look like a conventional research record, and do not
undersell the real record by apologizing for it. State the M.M. and the lack of classroom teaching
as plain facts, then let The Composer Compass and the field's own newness carry the argument for fit.
