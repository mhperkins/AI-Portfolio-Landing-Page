Pre-comp cards open one at a time

In the Compass Desk demo, opening a Pre-comp card now closes the one already open, so a zone never stacks two long cards. The demo is standalone: the site does not link to it.

270e4d8 main · pushed demo/compass-desk-demo.html Not browser-verified

The change

  • card() closes every other open card in the zone, then opens the clicked one.
  • Clicking the open card still closes it.
  • Applies to both Pre-comp zones: Concerto and Sonata.
if (el.classList.contains('is-closed'))
  el.closest('.dk-zbody')
    .querySelectorAll('.dk-card:not(.is-closed)')
    .forEach(c => c.classList.add('is-closed'));
el.classList.toggle('is-closed');
