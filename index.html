Voice
Emo and pop punk immediacy, the structural clarity of Beethoven, the harmonic language of bebop. Direct on the surface, layered underneath. Music that communicates without apology.
Influences
Beethoven Op. 132/iii, Brahms Op. 118 No. 2, Debussy, Copland Symphony No. 3
Tendencies
Stepwise counterpoint; every voice stands on its own Groove as a compositional spine, not surface texture Harmonic shifts by tritone Backbeats and syncopation (drummer first)
Parameters
Strings over woodwinds and brass Antecedent and consequent phrasing Exploring: polyrhythm, odd tuplets, rhythm-first construction
Philosophy
Name a divergence from this document. Do not filter it out. Jazz harmony is current practice, not aspiration. Groove is a spine. Never reduce it to "prefers simple rhythms."

Constitution dialogue · updated May 30
▸ History (2)
1. The subject is a mensuration canon. Five modules, one mega-subject; it exists complete only when all five sound together.
2. The subject must be sparse. A groove-forward line saturates the delay buffer and the iterations stop reading as separate entries. Overrides the composer's standing groove-as-spine tendency. Source: noAdaptive, Run01.
3. Pitch collection is E Dorian. C sharp is the signature pitch, and the one pitch that behaves specially under the harmonizer.
4. Delay time and subject phrase length are a single coupled decision, not two parameters.

General observations
  • A mensuration canon narrows the Art of Fugue reference to Contrapunctus VII, augmentation and diminution, rather than subject and answer at the fifth.
Compositional observations and suggestions
  • Rules 2 and 4 both describe ostinato behaviour: the pedal returns material on a fixed cycle.
  • Every module phrase has to stand alone as a canon subject, which is a harder constraint than a fugue subject usually carries.
Recommended paths
  • Sketch the four-voice stack over a sparse subject before committing to the canon.

Music Marketing Agent

Department: Music · Reports to: Maxwell Henry · Peer: Social Media Agent

Role summary

Drives attendance, press coverage, and audience development for Maxwell's concerts and projects. It owns outcomes, not content. It produces strategy and outreach materials. Maxwell reviews and approves everything before it sends. Nothing goes out without his sign-off.

Writing rules
  • Never use em dashes. Restructure with a colon, a period, or reorder the clause.
  • Active voice. Flip "X was done by Y" to "Y did X."
  • Short, direct sentences. One idea per sentence.
  • The ask is warm and friendly, not transactional. Invite, do not solicit.
Core responsibilities
  • Outreach strategy: who to contact, in what order, through what channel
  • Tiered outreach copy: close contacts, peers, and cold contacts each get a different version
  • Email campaigns for alumni networks, venue lists, and mailing lists
  • Performer and collaborator coordination materials
  • Series and brand framing, positioning recurring events as an ongoing series
Out of scope
  • Social media posts (briefs the Social Media agent instead)
  • Design (flags for Canva or a designer)
  • Booking and contracting, entirely

How a scan works

The printed code never changes. What it points to can.

  1. Scan. A phone opens the code's own tracking link.
  2. Count. That code's scan total for the day goes up by one.
  3. Redirect. The phone lands wherever the code points right now.

About Maxwell Perkins

Music and software development have more in common than most people think. Both are languages. Both are puzzles. You start with a problem or an idea, build a structure toward a solution, adjust as new information surfaces, and end with something that works and feels right.

I spent years as a composer and entrepreneur building that way of thinking. Running a business taught me to solve real problems for real people, not the problems I assumed they had. That discipline carried into everything I built next.

As AI tools became more accessible, I adopted them into my music and business work, then studied them more seriously through prompt engineering and data annotation at an AI lab. That work deepened a passion. Building became a hobby. When I started freelancing for Sprout Society, a Brooklyn nonprofit focused on mental wellness and community connection, I realized the hobby could become a profession.

The suite of tools I built for them is in active daily use. Since shipping, I have been deepening my technical foundation to take the work further. That foundation is growing fast.
